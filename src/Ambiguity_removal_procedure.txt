Disambiguation:

1. In the network feed (data generation):

Step 1: Find t_star = peak time |E(t)|. Resolved ambiguity: None (preparation for the next steps).
Step 2: Multiply the field by exp(-i * phase in t_star). Resolved ambiguity: Global phase.
Step 3 (conditional): If most of the energy (|E|^2) is at t > t_star -> flip in time + conjugate, then again Step 2. Resolved ambiguity: Time direction (flip+conj); Global phase (again, after flip).
Step 4: Divide the field by its norm (norm = 1). Resolved ambiguity: Global scale/amplitude.

Steps not performed in the feed:
• Time shift: Not resolved.
• E vs. E* (conjugate without flip): Not explicitly solved

2. On the network output (evaluation of the retrived pulses):

• Check three bases: E, E*, flip(E)* (conj; flip+conj)
• shift by |E| (time shift)
• (L1 only) phi search (global phase)
• choose the best base

question:

If you had to say which types of ambiguities have the lowest chance of appearing at the network output (and therefore probably do not require correction, with a high probability), what would they be?

Answer:

Lowest (needs least correction):

1. Scale / norm — network trained on norm=1

2. Flip+conj (time direction) — partially canonical in dataset

Medium:

3. E vs. E* — not resolved in feed

Highest (almost always needs correction in estimation):

4. Time shift — not resolved in feed
5. Global phase — FROG is blind to it; required for L1.
